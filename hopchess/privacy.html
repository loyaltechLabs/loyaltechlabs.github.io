HopChess Privacy Policy

Loyal Tech Labs · Effective 7 November 2026

In short: HopChess does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone.

Who we are

HopChess is a chess puzzle game published on Google Play by Loyal Tech Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. HopChess does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details, and it has no sign-in. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember your game, HopChess saves the following on your phone only:

  • Your progress: levels reached and beaten, best times and hops, and attempts per level.
  • Your play statistics and achievements (for example, total hops, captures and days played).
  • Your settings, such as sounds, vibration and board highlights.

This data never leaves your device through HopChess. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Android backup

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include HopChess's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. This backup is run by Android and Google under your own account; we never receive or have access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Fonts

HopChess displays its text using fonts from Google Fonts, which your phone downloads through Google Play services the first time the app needs them. That download is handled by Google Play services on your device, not by HopChess, and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. No personal information from HopChess is included in it.

Permissions

HopChess asks for one permission: vibration, used for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Children

HopChess is a puzzle game suitable for a general audience. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of HopChess changes what happens to your information (for example, by adding optional ads), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released, and describe the change in the app's Google Play listing.
